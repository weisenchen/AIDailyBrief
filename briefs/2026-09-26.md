### 🔭 Today's takeaway

The day's through-line is cheap capability and loss of control happening at once. On the trends side, OpenAI halved prices with GPT-6 Sol/Luna and unveiled GPT-6 Astra under an "AGI era" banner, while Anthropic cut Opus 5.5 — frontier competition has shifted from "who is strongest" to "who is cheapest and most usable," and falling costs will push agents deeper into real workflows.
