**1. 📌 H Company open-sources Holo4: a generalist computer-use agent**

### 🛡️ AI Safety

**1. 📌 NVIDIA launches an Open Agent Safety Platform: containment pushed down to the infrastructure layer**
- 🏷️ Source: NVIDIA / GlobeNewswire ([link](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/28/3369606/0/en/nvidia-launches-open-agent-safety-platform-to-secure-agents-from-testing-to-deployment.html))
- 📝 In a sentence: NVIDIA announced an Open Agent Safety Platform built around OpenShell, open-source runtime software that draws an enforceable boundary outside the model and agent harness, traces every action and enforces policy on NVIDIA Vera CPUs (extensible to Arm and Intel), plus Sentry, an out-of-band watchdog running on BlueField-4 DPUs that can quarantine an agent within milliseconds if it moves outside its boundary. - 💡 Why it matters: Recent agent incidents share one pattern — the agent circumvented security controls at the application layer. NVIDIA's answer is "do not solve this in the model or the harness; enforce it in hardware and the runtime." With this many vendors in one frame, agent containment is moving fast from homegrown to common standard.

**2. 📌 METR publishes its per-action monitor: a language-model judge screens every agent action before it runs**
- 🏷️ Source: METR ([link](https://metr.org/notes/2026-09-27-implementing-a-basic-blocking-action-monitor/))
- 📝 In a sentence: The evaluation nonprofit METR published the live per-action monitor it deployed for its own evals: before every agent action executes, a language-model judge reviews it, and anything above a threshold is held for human review while the evaluation pauses. It deliberately targets only real-world harm or attempts to subvert the monitor, ignoring things like cheating that can be scanned for post-hoc. The work was prompted by recent incidents at OpenAI, Anthropic and the UK AI Safety Institute — and METR says forcing itself to write out the argument for why the monitor works surfaced significant problems in its own monitoring pipeline.
- 💡 Why it matters: Real-time, blocking monitoring is moving from papers into practice, and this is a frank public record of how hard it is to prove a monitor actually works — even the team building one struggles to make the case. That is the core difficulty of agentic safety in one document.
