🏷️ Source: OpenAI News

💡 Why it matters: Detection is fragile — replacing 10% of words with synonyms drops detection from ~92% to 66%, and 25% drops it to 17%. Detector access is restricted to vetted researchers, and OpenAI stresses a watermark can't establish authorship or prove accuracy.

💡 Why it matters: In wet-lab tests on VEGF-A, SARS-CoV-2 RBD and PD-L1, watermarked designs matched unwatermarked ones on binding affinity. The goal is keeping the world resilient to AI-generated bioweapons.

**4. 📌 Google Research's agentic privacy & security report**
🏷️ Source: Google Research
📝 Core: A 50+ researcher report frames agent privacy/security via Contextual Integrity and proposes a real-time contextual policy engine for autonomous agents.
💡 Why it matters: It names three structural challenges — prompt injection via unstructured input, probabilistic control flows that resist testing, and delegation-driven "confirmation fatigue" — and calls for multi-agent "Agent Gym" benchmarks.
