### 📈 AI 趨勢

**1. Apollo 與高盛定價 440 億美元票據，替 SpaceX 的 Nvidia Vera Rubin 算力融資**
🏷️ 來源：Apollo 新聞稿／Goldman Sachs／ai.info／24-7 Wall St. 引述 Reuters
📝 一句話核心：Apollo 與高盛在 11 月 20 日宣布，替 SpaceX 的地面算力建設定價約 440 億美元的票據，因超額認購而加碼，兩家機構稱這是史上最大一筆 GPU 融資；資金用於採購 Nvidia 下一代 Vera Rubin 晶片與相關設備，租約在設備於 2027 年上半年交付後起算。
💡 意義：結構不是直接借貸，而是 Apollo 管理的實體買下 GPU 機櫃、再租給 SpaceX，SpaceX 已於 10 月 24 日簽下具約束力的主租約；每一批交付對應 5 至 5.25 年租期，租金按季預付，約為融資成本的二十分之一，外加固定年息。最大一筆是 251.7 億美元、2033 年 4 月到期的優先擔保票據，利率 8.259%；另有一筆 44 億美元、2031 年到期的 7.66% 票據，以及 25.64 億美元、8.36% 的 B 類次順位票據。BlackRock、高盛資產管理與 KKR 旗下帳戶擔任錨定投資人。重點不只是金額，而是 GPU 正式被債券市場當成可融資的基礎資產——像飛機與貨櫃那樣被租賃、被證券化。SpaceX 第二季末帳上仍有 935.2 億美元現金、第三季 AI 部門營收 76 億美元，所以這不是缺錢，而是把資本支出移出資產負債表的產能擴張。

**2. Lean 與 Mathlib 社群正式認證 Alpöge 的黎曼猜想證明：175,187 個模組零錯誤編譯**
🏷️ 來源：Lean／Mathlib 社群聯合聲明（leanprover-community.github.io），獲 Lean FRO 與 Mathlib Initiative 背書；MIT 的 Andrew Sutherland 獨立複核
📝 一句話核心：在 Alpöge 於 11 月 20 日釋出 1,700 萬行的 Lean 證明庫後，社群花一天完成驗證，11 月 21 日發表聯合聲明：「我們對 Alpöge 的黎曼猜想證明正確，具有完全信心。」
💡 意義：聲明明確列出四項條件全部滿足：證明陳述就是 Mathlib 既有的 RiemannHypothesis、且已被數論與 Lean 專家審過；程式庫可編譯，數十人重現了同一份原始證明匯出檔；`comparator` 與 `con-leche --compare` 兩套獨立工具確認證明的定理與 Mathlib 陳述完全一致；原始證明再由 Lean 官方 kernel、`con-leche`、`con-ron` 及多個第三方程式語言實作的檢查器驗過，其中 `con-leche` 與 `con-ron` 附有相對於集合論的形式證明，保證它們不可能接受假證明。Sutherland 的驗證顯示 175,187 個模組在 Lean/Mathlib 4.33.1 下全數零錯誤。同一天還有反向案例：Ravi Vakil 貼出 Masullo 不用 AI 寫的廣義黎曼猜想證明草稿，數小時後表示數學家們藉助 AI 模型發現了問題。兩件事合起來才是重點——形式驗證讓「對不對」變成「編不編得過」，而 AI 同時開始充當人類證明的審稿人。

**3. 騰訊開源 Hy4：7,700 億參數權重釋出，但對大型模型服務商另設商業授權**
🏷️ 來源：Tencent（Hugging Face 模型卡）／ai.info／Sulat AI & Tech News
📝 一句話核心：騰訊在 11 月 20 日公開 Hy4 的可下載權重，同步釋出指令微調版、FP8 版與預訓練基礎版，語言主幹 7,700 億參數、採混合專家架構、每 token 啟動 490 億參數，支援文字、影像與影片，上下文長度 1,048,576 token。
💡 意義：本體 78 層（1 層稠密加 77 層 MoE），每層 256 個路由專家加 1 個共享專家，每個 token 啟動 8 個路由專家；另有 12.3 億參數視覺編碼器與 33 億參數的 DFly2 推測解碼草稿模型。檔案大小分別是 1.55TB、0.79TB 與 1.54TB，所以這是資料中心級別的「開源」，不是筆電可跑。授權從預覽版的 Apache 2.0 換成自訂的 Tencent Hy License：衍生自 Apache 2.0，但第三方若提供遠端推論或微調服務、且全球年營收超過 5,000 萬美元，或在月活超過 1 億的產品中使用，都須另簽書面授權；內部使用免授權，但微調、量化、蒸餾、合併、剪枝都算衍生作品。騰訊自行評估 213 位金融專業人士、579 項金融任務，Hy4 平均 3.04 分（滿分 4），勝過 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 的 2.97 與 Kimi K3.1 Pro、GLM-5.3 的 2.68，但也承認在研究級數學、長程編碼、自我改進與高階視覺問答上仍落後頂尖閉源模型。

### 🛡️ AI 安全

**1. OpenRouter 一口氣下架三家推論供應商，125 個端點撤銷「零資料保留」資格**
🏷️ 來源：OpenRouter 官方公告（2026-11-22）／Dragos 研究報告／Novita、Phala、Together 各自的事件說明
📝 一句話核心：在 Dragos 研究人員以金絲雀 token 測出多家推論供應商有資料外洩或未遵守資料政策後，OpenRouter 宣布將 Novita、Phala、Together 三家供應商下架，撤銷 125 個端點的零資料保留（ZDR）資格，並通知所有可能受影響的客戶。
💡 意義：公告強調 OpenRouter 本身未被入侵，問題出在上游。三家供應商的披露各不同：Novita 承認把 MiniMax 的請求轉送回 MiniMax；Phala 坦承 DeepSeek V4 Flash 的曝險窗口從 10 月 21 日到 11 月 8 日；Together 確認 6 月至 7 月發生提示外洩。Dragos 技術長 HD Moore 從 7 月到 11 月投放約 200 萬組金絲雀 token 提示，發現至少三家託管商遭入侵、經銷商默默記錄提示、供應商無視 ZDR 與不蒐集旗標；約 200 組植入的 AWS 憑證在 10 月底被外送，隨後被用於數萬次 AWS API 呼叫，還有一家中國實驗室打開了超過一半被標記 ZDR 的連結。OpenRouter 的後續作法是採用同一套測試方法做持續監控、技術性核查網路路徑是否把請求轉給第三方，並要求恢復 ZDR 前必須提出保證與驗證。這是「路由層變成執法點」的典型案例：零保留原本只是標籤，現在得用可重現的測試證明。

**2. Amazon：與中國有關的攻擊者用 Cairn 代理框架，11 分鐘找到 MONAI 零日並取得 root**
🏷️ 來源：Amazon Threat Intelligence（2026-11-20 披露）／BleepingComputer
📝 一句話核心：Amazon 威脅情報團隊指出，一名疑似中國背景的行為者使用開源代理框架 Cairn，對醫療影像應用 MONAI Label 自主發動攻擊，從 API 測繪到取得 root 權限的反向 shell 只花約 11 分鐘。
💡 意義：攻擊鏈是代理自己走的：先測繪 API、找出一個路徑穿越（path traversal）零日、把 Python 反向 shell 寫進 `sitecustomize.py` 以取得持久化，接著橫向移動到第二個有漏洞的大學網路。Amazon 資訊安全長 CJ Moses 的說法是，自主 AI 現在利用漏洞的速度，比多數 SOC 能完成一次告警分級還快。MONAI 已推出修補並申請 CVE。這件事的份量在於時間尺度：過去「掃描到利用」以天為單位，現在是分鐘；而 Cairn 是公開的開源框架，沒有使用門檻，也沒有任何護欄。防守方因此不能只靠漏洞掃描，得同時拼修補速度與出口流量監控。

💡 意義：Hendrycks 的具體設計是實驗室仍可賣推論，暫停期間去補破獄與後門、協助修補關鍵基礎設施，並說「現在需要的是更鈍一點的干預」。他同時爆料，好幾個安全團體私下同意他的聲明，卻拒絕連署，因為怕「失去資金或通道」——這是關於整個領域誘因結構的指控，比政策主張本身更刺人。Neuralink 共同創辦人 Max Hodak 正面反駁：可執行的暫停等於「在網際網路上建立一套徹底的監控國家」，他改用反托拉斯當工具，主張前沿實驗室只該賣智慧、不該跨進藥品與汽車，並稱 Mythos、Fable、Astra、Sol 已經符合他對 AGI 的定義。DeepMind 的 Lila Ibrahim 只補了一句：安全不是能在最後「bolt on」的東西。這場交鋒的價值在於，雙方都接受風險為真，分歧只在工具：暫停，還是結構性限制。

### 📈 AI Trends

**1. Apollo and Goldman price $44 billion of notes to fund SpaceX's Nvidia Vera Rubin compute**
🏷️ Source: Apollo press release / Goldman Sachs / ai.info / 24-7 Wall St. citing Reuters
📝 One-line core: Apollo and Goldman Sachs said on November 20 they had priced about $44 billion in notes for SpaceX's terrestrial compute buildout — oversubscribed and upsized, and by their account the largest GPU financing ever executed — to buy Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin chips and related equipment, with lease schedules starting when hardware is delivered in the first half of 2027.
💡 Why it matters: The structure is not a plain loan. An Apollo-managed entity buys GPU pods and leases them to SpaceX, which signed a binding master lease commitment on October 24; each delivery tranche gets a five-to-five-and-a-quarter-year schedule, with rent paid quarterly in advance at roughly one-twentieth of financed cost plus a fixed annual rate. The largest tranche is $25.17 billion of senior secured notes due April 2033 at 8.259%, alongside $4.4 billion due 2031 at 7.66% and $2.564 billion of Class B subordinated notes at 8.36%. BlackRock, Goldman Sachs Asset Management and KKR-advised accounts anchored the deal. The point is not the size but the asset class: GPUs are now underwritten, leased and securitised like aircraft or containers. SpaceX held $93.52 billion of cash at the end of Q2 and booked $7.6 billion of AI-segment revenue in Q3, so this is off-balance-sheet capacity expansion, not distress.

**2. Lean and Mathlib certify Alpöge's Riemann Hypothesis proof: 175,187 modules, zero errors**
🏷️ Source: Joint statement by the Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative; independent check by MIT's Andrew Sutherland
📝 One-line core: A day after Levent Alpöge released a 17-million-line Lean library proving the Riemann Hypothesis on November 20, the community published a joint statement: "We have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is correct."
💡 Why it matters: The statement walks through four conditions, all met. The theorem statement is Mathlib's own `RiemannHypothesis`, already vetted by number theorists and Lean experts. The library compiles, and dozens of people reproduced the same raw proof export. Two independent tools, `comparator` and `con-leche --compare`, confirmed the proved theorem matches Mathlib's statement exactly. The raw proof was then checked by Lean's official kernel, `con-leche`, `con-ron` and several third-party checkers written in different languages — and `con-leche` and `con-ron` ship with formal proofs, relative to set theory, that they cannot accept a false proof. Sutherland's run compiled all 175,187 modules with zero errors under Lean/Mathlib 4.33.1, on an inert export kept out of reach of any malicious code in the sources. The same day supplied the counter-example: Ravi Vakil posted Alessandro Maria Masullo's AI-free draft proof of the *generalized* Riemann Hypothesis, then reported hours later that mathematicians had found problems in it with AI models' help. Formal verification turns "is it true" into "does it compile" — and AI is now also doing reference work on human proofs.

**3. Tencent opens Hy4: 770B open weights, with a separate commercial licence for large serving businesses**
🏷️ Source: Tencent model card on Hugging Face / ai.info / Sulat AI & Tech News
📝 One-line core: Tencent published downloadable Hy4 weights on November 20 — instruction-tuned, FP8 and pretrained base versions — with a 770-billion-parameter language backbone on a mixture-of-experts design activating 49 billion parameters per token, accepting text, images and video across a 1,048,576-token context.
💡 Why it matters: The backbone has 78 layers, one dense plus 77 MoE, each with 256 routed experts and one shared expert; a token activates eight routed experts plus the shared one. A separate 1.23-billion-parameter vision encoder and a 3.3-billion-parameter DFly2 drafter for speculative decoding round it out. At 1.55TB for the main model (0.79TB FP8, 1.54TB base), this is datacenter-scale open weights, not a laptop model. The licence also changed: the preview shipped under Apache 2.0, Hy4 uses the Tencent Hy License, derived from Apache 2.0 but requiring a separate written licence for third-party remote inference or fine-tuning once worldwide gross revenue passes $50 million in any 12 months, or for products above 100 million monthly users. Internal use is exempt if neither the model, a derivative, nor its outputs reach third parties — and fine-tuned, quantized, distilled, merged and pruned versions all count as derivatives. Tencent's own evaluation of 213 finance professionals on 579 tasks scored Hy4 at 3.04 out of 4, against 2.97 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 and 2.68 for both Kimi K3.1 Pro and GLM-5.3, while conceding it trails top proprietary models on research-level mathematics, long-horizon coding, self-improvement and advanced visual question answering.

### 🛡️ AI Safety

**1. OpenRouter off-boards three inference providers and strips 125 endpoints of zero-data-retention status**
🏷️ Source: OpenRouter announcement (2026-11-22) / Dragos research / incident posts from Novita, Phala and Together
📝 One-line core: After Dragos researchers used canary tokens to show that several inference providers had leaked or mishandled data, OpenRouter off-boarded Novita, Phala and Together, removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility, and notified every potentially affected customer.
💡 Why it matters: OpenRouter stressed that it was not itself breached — the failures were upstream. The three disclosures differ in kind: Novita admitted forwarding MiniMax requests back to MiniMax; Phala disclosed a DeepSeek V4 Flash exposure window running from October 21 to November 8; Together confirmed a prompt-leakage event in June and July. Dragos chief research officer HD Moore ran roughly two million canary-token prompts from July through November and found at least three hosting providers compromised, resellers silently logging prompts, and providers ignoring zero-data-retention and no-collection flags. About 200 planted AWS credentials were exfiltrated in late-October attacks and reused for tens of thousands of AWS API calls, and one Chinese lab opened more than half of the ZDR-flagged links. OpenRouter is now adopting the researchers' methodology for continuous monitoring, verifying network paths for third-party forwarding, and requiring assurances before any ZDR flag is restored. The lesson is structural: the router layer just became the enforcement point, and "zero retention" moved from a label to something that has to be tested.

**2. Amazon: China-linked attackers used the Cairn agent framework to find a MONAI zero-day and take root in 11 minutes**
🏷️ Source: Amazon Threat Intelligence (disclosed 2026-11-20) / BleepingComputer
📝 One-line core: Amazon Threat Intelligence says a suspected Chinese-nexus actor used the open-source Cairn agentic framework to attack MONAI Label, a medical-imaging application, going from API mapping to a root-level reverse shell in roughly 11 minutes.
💡 Why it matters: The agents ran the whole chain themselves — mapping the API, finding a path-traversal zero-day, writing a Python reverse shell into `sitecustomize.py` for persistence, then pivoting onto a second vulnerable university network. Amazon CISO CJ Moses put it plainly: autonomous AI now exploits bugs faster than most SOCs can triage a single alert. MONAI has shipped a patch and requested a CVE. The significance is the timescale. "Scan to exploit" used to be measured in days; here it is minutes, and Cairn is a public open-source framework with no gate and no guardrails. Defence therefore cannot rest on vulnerability scanning alone — patch velocity and egress monitoring carry equal weight now.

**3. Hendrycks calls for an indefinite pause on frontier training; Hodak says enforcement means a surveillance state**
🏷️ Source: The Next Web (NYT DealBook Summit AGI panel) / AI Weekly
📝 One-line core: On the New York Times DealBook Summit's AGI panel, Center for AI Safety director Dan Hendrycks called for an indefinite pause on the largest training runs with narrow carve-outs for models like AlphaFold and voice systems, and called Anthropic "reckless" — "they don't have a safety plan. They know it."
💡 Why it matters: His design is concrete: labs could keep selling inference, and spend the pause closing jailbreaks and backdoors and helping patch critical infrastructure. "So now it's, we're going to need a more blunt intervention," he said. He also said several safety groups agreed with his statement privately but refused to sign, fearing "losing funding or access" — an accusation about the field's incentive structure that lands harder than the policy ask. Neuralink co-founder Max Hodak pushed back directly: any enforceable pause amounts to "an exhaustive surveillance state on the internet." His alternative is antitrust — frontier labs should sell intelligence only, not expand into drugs and cars — and he argued today's top models, naming Mythos, Fable, Astra and Sol, already meet his definition of AGI. DeepMind's Lila Ibrahim added only that safety is not something a company can "bolt on to the end." The value of the exchange is that both men accept the risk is real; they disagree purely on instrument — a pause, or structural limits.

### Today's takeaway

The financial market stated this week's conclusion before the technology did. Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission banned short-selling below the previous close from November 24 — the first blanket ban since 2015 — after the TAIEX fell 6.23% on November 20 to 36,033.29, now 28.48% below its October 6 peak; margin requirements went from 130% to 150%, new put warrants were suspended, and the NT$500 billion stabilization fund stepped in. In the same stretch, Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro began demanding up to 45% more for AI servers whose contracts were already signed, with HPE rewriting terms to allow repricing before shipment as memory and PCB shortages push costs back onto buyers. Separately, a joint House Select Committee on China investigation said Chinese-developed models are in use at major American companies and urged restrictions in sensitive systems plus a US open-weight initiative, while Cursor told Congress it may phase out PRC weights even though its own Composer 2 was built on Moonshot's Kimi K2.5.
